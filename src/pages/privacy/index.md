## 2. Hosting and technical infrastructure

This website is delivered using Microsoft Azure. The domain name and DNS services are provided by STRATO.

When you access this website, technical information may be processed by the infrastructure required to deliver and secure the website. This processing is necessary to provide the website, maintain its security, and detect and prevent abuse or attacks. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable website.

STRATO is used for domain registration and DNS services. Microsoft Azure is used to provide the website's hosting and delivery infrastructure.

## 3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party tracking services.

The website does not use cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes.

No user profiles or behavioral tracking are created.

## 4. Theme preference

The website allows you to choose between a light and a dark theme.

Your theme preference is stored locally in your browser using `localStorage`. This information is stored only on your device and is not used to identify or track you. The theme preference is not transmitted to us.

You can remove this locally stored information through your browser settings.

## 5. External content
